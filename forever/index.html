WoW: Forever launch prep

What we know so far, and what it means for us. We're going Horde on the PvP ruleset.

Launch: Wednesday, November 4, 2026 at 3:00 PM PST

Key dates

  • Now to Oct 21PSTBeta. Access comes with the Skyborne Epic Pack or the Forever Collection, plus opt-in invites. The level cap started at 20 and is now 30. A Server Slam test is planned, date not announced.
  • Oct 27 to Nov 3PSTEarly name reservation, first come first served, for the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher. Names have two parts (first name and surname) and are unique across the whole region, so grab yours early if you can.
  • Nov 4, 3:00 PMPST, no head startLaunch. Everyone starts fresh. Nothing has been announced about bringing TBC or Era characters over.
  • Dec 9Raids open. See below.
  • This winterHardcore ruleset, date not announced.

Where we'll be: Horde, PvP ruleset

Forever has no realms. You pick a ruleset instead (Normal, PvP, Roleplaying, later Hardcore), and each ruleset is one big shared world split into layers. What that means for you:

  • Make your character on the PvP ruleset, Horde side. That's where we are.
  • On PvP you can only have characters in one faction, so don't start an Alliance alt there.
  • Characters can't move between rulesets, and you can't group across them. A character on Normal can never raid with us.
  • Blizzard may briefly block new characters on the bigger faction to keep PvP balanced. If Horde creation is locked at launch, check with an officer before you roll anywhere else.

Horde races and classes

Still the same nine classes. New on our side: Orc Mage, Troll Warlock, and Undead Paladin (Undead are the only Horde paladins), plus the Skyborne, a new race that can pick either faction. Skyborne start on Zephras Isle (levels 1 to 12) and need the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher.

Talent trees are reworked with a new 16-point milestone. Blessing of Kings, Divine Spirit, and Improved Mark of the Wild are now baseline, so raid spots won't hinge on who brings those buffs.

Leveling to 60

The level cap is 60, with no flying and no level scaling. There are over 1,000 new quests, new zones (Mount Hyjal, Shen'dralas, the Riverglades for the mid-30s to mid-40s, and Zephras Isle), and nine new dungeons:

Raids

Raids open December 9, about five weeks after launch. That's our window to level, gear up in dungeons, and lock in a roster.

  • Every tier is planned to have a 10-player and a 20-player raid. 40-player raids only show up in some tiers.
  • One difficulty per raid. No heroic mode, no flex sizing.
  • New tier sets, and a legendary.

Loot

  • GDKP is banned in Forever.
  • An epic raid drop's appearance only unlocks for whoever it binds to. Blizzard is leaving loot decisions to the raid group.
  • Our loot council rules carry over as they are. Read the Rules.

Gear and stat changes

  • Hit is one stat now across melee, ranged, and spells. Crit works the same way.
  • Weapon skill on gear is weaker, and new stats lower your chance to be parried or dodged.
  • Bonus healing also gives a third as much bonus damage. Caster weapons carry spell damage and healing.
  • Dungeon boss uniques are blue quality.
  • Over 600 new profession recipes, some dropped by dungeon bosses as blueprints.

Still unknown

Raid reset day and time, attunements, how loot is handed out in-game (master loot or otherwise), dual spec, population caps and layer hopping, and whether the game shows item level. This page gets updated as Blizzard announces them.
