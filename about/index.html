Captain's Log of <Blackwater Company>

About the Company

<Blackwater Company> is a pirate-themed roleplay guild sailing under Alliance colors on WoW: Forever's RP-PvP ruleset. We raid with focus and keep the good times that come from hanging out with your crew.

Our heading

We're live on World of Warcraft: Forever, Alliance side, on the RP-PvP ruleset.

Raids open December 9. Raid nights get set before then. Rulesets, races and classes, dungeons and raids are on the Forever page.

Past plunder

In The Burning Crusade Anniversary we cleared Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon within weeks of forming.

The roster isn't new to this. We took a Realm First! Champion of the Frozen Wastes in Wrath of the Lich King Classic, and the ranks include multiple Rank 1 players from Vanilla, Wrath, and Mists of Pandaria.

Shares & culture

High-ticket loot (tier, weapons, trinkets) goes by loot council on the priority Performance > Attendance > Guild Activity. Everything else is rolled. We're competitive about raid spots and relaxed about everything else.

The full rules live in the Ship's Articles. Read them before you raid.

Signing on

There's no application form yet. If you want to sign on, make an Alliance character on RP-PvP and message @ivorycrayon on Discord. We don't bite (in chat).
