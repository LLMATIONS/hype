About us

<hype> is a semi-hardcore progression guild built around focused, efficient raiding without sacrificing the good times that come from hanging out with your team.

Where we're headed

Next stop: World of Warcraft: Forever, launching November 4, 2026.

We've stepped away from The Burning Crusade Anniversary. We're going Horde on the PvP ruleset, and raids open December 9. Raid nights get locked in closer to then. Everything else we know is on the launch prep page.

What we've cleared

In The Burning Crusade Anniversary we cleared Karazhan, Gruul's Lair, and Magtheridon within weeks of forming.

The roster isn't new to this. We took a Realm First! Champion of the Frozen Wastes in Wrath of the Lich King Classic, and the ranks include multiple Rank 1 players from Vanilla, Wrath, and Mists of Pandaria.

Loot & culture

High-ticket loot (tier, weapons, trinkets) goes by loot council on the priority Performance > Attendance > Guild Activity. Everything else is rolled. We're competitive about raid spots and relaxed about everything else.

The full house rules live on the Rules page. Read them before you raid.

Recruiting

Applications are closed until we're closer to launch. If you want in for Forever, message @ivorycrayon on Discord and we'll keep you in mind. We don't bite (in chat).
